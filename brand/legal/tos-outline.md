- Calls are routed through third-party telephony providers (Twilio)
- The service is provided for legitimate business communication purposes only
- call-use is a tool — users are responsible for the content and purpose of their calls

## 3. Account Registration and Security

- Users must provide accurate, current, and complete registration information
- Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their credentials
- Users must notify call-use immediately of any unauthorized access
- call-use reserves the right to require identity verification
- One account per user/organization unless otherwise authorized

## 4. Acceptable Use Policy

### 4.1 Permitted Uses
- Legitimate business communications with proper recipient consent
- Appointment scheduling and reminders
- Customer service and support callbacks
- Order confirmations and delivery notifications
- Authorized sales and marketing calls (with PEWC)
- Internal business communications
- Accessibility assistance (making calls for persons with disabilities)

### 4.2 Prohibited Uses

**Illegal Activity:**
- Calls without prior express consent as required by the TCPA
- Calls to numbers on the National Do Not Call Registry without valid exemption
- Caller ID spoofing with intent to defraud or cause harm
- Fraud, phishing, social engineering, or impersonation
- Debt collection in violation of the FDCPA
- Calls that violate any federal, state, or local law

**Harmful Activity:**
- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or abuse
- Calls intended to annoy, alarm, or disturb
- Hate speech or discriminatory communications
- Calls on behalf of illegal enterprises
- Distribution of malware or exploits via voice social engineering

**Deceptive Activity:**
- Impersonating a specific real person without their consent
- Failing to disclose the AI nature of the caller
- Misrepresenting the identity or purpose of the call
- Creating deepfake voice calls to manipulate or deceive
- Misrepresenting affiliation with any person, company, or government entity

**Spam and Bulk Abuse:**
- Mass unsolicited calling campaigns without consent
- Exceeding reasonable call volume limits
- Calls designed primarily to tie up phone lines
- Sequential or random number dialing

**Regulated Communications (Without Proper Compliance):**
- Healthcare communications without HIPAA compliance
- Financial services communications without applicable compliance (GLBA, etc.)
- Political robocalls without required disclaimers
- Emergency services or 911 abuse

### 4.3 Calling-Specific Obligations

Users must:
- Obtain and document prior express consent (written for marketing) for all calls
- Disclose AI nature within the first few seconds of every call
- Display accurate, non-spoofed caller ID
- Respect calling hours (8 AM - 9 PM called party's local time)
- Honor all opt-out/Do Not Call requests within 10 business days
- Scrub call lists against the National DNC Registry at least every 31 days (for telemarketing)
- Comply with all applicable state recording consent laws
- Maintain consent documentation for a minimum of 5 years
- Provide a mechanism for called parties to opt out during the call
