## Priority Tiers

### Tier 1 — Do on Launch Day
- Hacker News (Show HN)
- Twitter/X thread
- Reddit (r/Python, r/MachineLearning, r/LangChain)
- Product Hunt
- LinkedIn
- Dev.to article
- LiveKit Discord, LangChain Discord

### Tier 2 — Within First Week
- Newsletter submissions (TLDR AI, Ben's Bites, Python Weekly, Console.dev)
- Podcast pitches (Latent Space, Practical AI, Talk Python)
- DMs to top 5 Twitter influencers
- Remaining Reddit posts
- Remaining Discord/Slack communities

### Tier 3 — Within First Month
- YouTube creator outreach
- Blog/media pitches (only if traction warrants it)
- Conference talk submissions (if applicable)
- Follow-up pitches to anyone who didn't respond
